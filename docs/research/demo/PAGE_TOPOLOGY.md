2. Introduction — breadcrumb, page title, italic proposition, deadline copy, and a two-column participation summary.

5. Logistics — three definition-list rows.
6. FAQ — nine question/answer rows, contact copy, and two CTA buttons.

All flow sections share `max-width: 85rem`, `padding-inline: 1.5rem` on mobile and `2.5rem` at the `md` breakpoint. The card, checklist, and logistics grids collapse to one column below their respective `sm`/`md` breakpoints.
